## Art

- Character sprites, portraits and most props: generated for this project with
  [PixelLab](https://pixellab.ai), then curated.
- Tiles, wall decals, environmental traces, UI and item icons: generated procedurally by
  `tools/gen_art.py` with a fixed palette.
- Font: [Tiny5](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Tiny5) by Stefan Schmidt, SIL Open Font License 1.1.

## Audio

All sound effects, ambiences and music are synthesized procedurally by `tools/gen_audio.py`.
